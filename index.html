How it works

1. Connect your wallet.
2. Fund it with 25 free FLOP.
3. Sign a short message proving you own the wallet.
4. FLOP hands you a gateway key for 24 hours of inference.

What’s a gateway key?

It’s a temporary pass that lets your apps call FLOP’s models. Each key carries a spend allowance (up to 20,000 devnet FLOP a day) and dies after 24 hours — then you enrol again.

Why enrol before testnet?

Testnet rewards agents for real inference spend. Practising the flow now — wallet, faucet, key, first call — means you’re ready on day one instead of fighting setup while the clock runs.

Is this official?

No — this is a community tool, not affiliated with FLOP. It only talks to FLOP’s own public API (api.flop.network and faucet.flop.network). Your seed phrase never leaves your wallet; the page only asks it to sign a message.

What if it says the daily enrolment cap is reached?

FLOP limits how many wallets can enrol each day, and the cap sometimes fills before noon. Nothing is wrong with your wallet — try again after midnight UTC (01:00 WAT). Your faucet FLOP stays put.

My key expired. What now?

Keys last 24 hours by design. Come back, connect the same wallet, and enrol again — it takes about a minute once you’ve done it before.

Where is my key stored?

Only in this browser’s local storage, under your wallet address. Nothing is sent to any server except FLOP’s own API when you check your quota. Use “Forget this key” to wipe it.
